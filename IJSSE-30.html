About Journal

Promoting research, innovation and academic excellence.

The International Journal of Scientific and Social Excellence (IJSSE) is an academic journal website designed to support researchers, authors, reviewers and editors in the scholarly publishing process.

The journal provides a platform for sharing research in science, technology, engineering, education, management and social sciences.

Its mission is to encourage quality research, knowledge sharing, academic collaboration and responsible publication practices.

Author Guidelines

  • Submit original and unpublished research work.
  • Include a clear title and abstract.

  • Include references and citations.
  • Submit the copyright declaration where required.
  • Ensure all authors approve the manuscript.
